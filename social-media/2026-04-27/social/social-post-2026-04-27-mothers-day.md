## Instagram / Facebook Caption (Long Form)

Most Mother's Day gifts end up on a shelf.

A sourdough starter jar gets *fed*. Gets *named*. Gets passed down.

We bring starter jars to the Castell Street Market every Saturday — and they're one of the most meaningful things we make. Each one comes with simple, real-person feeding instructions (no baking experience needed). If your mom's already a baker, she gets a well-established culture with years of character built in.

We've had customers come back to tell us their moms have been keeping theirs going for over a year. One person told us their mom named hers.

We love knowing it has a name.

- **Cinnamon Brown Sugar** — the one people call "dangerous." Incredible French toast. Disappears fast.
- **Lemon Blueberry** — bright, tangy, feels like a treat every slice.
- **Orange Cranberry Walnut** — complex, cozy, pairs beautifully with soft cheese or just butter.
